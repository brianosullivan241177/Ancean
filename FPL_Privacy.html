Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 16 January 2026

FPL Deadline Tracker does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We believe in absolute privacy. The FPL Deadline Tracker app is designed to function entirely without the need for user registration or data input. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or IP address.

2. Log Data and Analytics

The app does not use any third-party analytics or tracking software. No data regarding your device usage or patterns is ever transmitted to us or any third parties.

3. Device Permissions

This app may request standard permissions (such as Notifications) solely to provide the core functionality of alerting you to FPL deadlines. Any settings related to these permissions are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not contain links to third-party websites or services that collect user data, other than standard system-level interactions required by your device's operating system (e.g., Apple App Store or Google Play Store services).

5. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any data, our application is safe for all ages and complies with the UK GDPR and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
